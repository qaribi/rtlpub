# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and specific. Welcome different languages, backgrounds
and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code; avoid harassment, threats,
discrimination, personal attacks and disclosure of private information.

Keep examples synthetic and lawful to share. Do not publish another person's
contact details or private content. Maintainers may remove harmful material,
close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement should be
proportionate and considerate of the people affected.

For concerns, contact the maintainer through an available private GitHub channel.
If none is available, request a private channel without naming affected people
or posting sensitive details. This is a project-specific conduct policy.
